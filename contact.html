Player-built roadmap

Help us build tools Whiteout Survival players actually want.

We are trying to make this site useful enough that players come back before events, upgrades, code checks, and alliance planning. If something feels missing, confusing, wrong, too shallow, or not worth using on mobile, we want to know.

This is a fan-made tool site. The best ideas should come from real players who know what wastes time in-game.

What to send us

  • Tools you wish existed for Whiteout Survival.
  • Event planning pain points, especially SvS, KOI, Frostfire, Bear Trap, Foundry, and alliance routines.
  • Wrong or outdated codes, rewards, event notes, or timeline assumptions.
  • Mobile usability problems, screenshots, and places where the site feels hard to use.
  • Ideas that would make you bookmark this site and come back weekly.

Suggested email format

Server or region, your player type, the feature or correction, why it matters, and any screenshot/source link that helps us verify it.
